# Q4 2026 Marketing Plan

Prepared by Jordan Ellis, Marketing Coordinator. Reviewed with Wally and Maya on
September 26.

**Total Q4 budget: $45,000**

| Line item | Budget |
|---|---|
| Holiday drink launch (signage, cups, samples) | $12,000 |
| Black Friday subscription promo | $9,500 |
| Rose City Books co-branded gift set | $8,000 |
| Paid social and search | $7,500 |
| RTD cold brew launch support | $5,500 |
| UGC campaign and contest prizes | $2,500 |

## Holiday Drinks

Two drinks, launching December 1 across all 18 locations.

**Spiced Maple Latte** is already on as a fall seasonal and we are carrying it
through the holidays rather than pulling it. It has outperformed last year's fall
seasonal by a good margin and there is no reason to kill something that is working
just because the calendar changed.

**Peppermint Mocha** returns December 1. Same recipe as last year. People asked
about it starting in early November last year, so we are putting "back December 1"
on the chalkboards from November 20 to capture that.

Launch support: new chalkboard art at every location (templates from me, drawn by
whoever has the best handwriting), holiday cup sleeves, and a sampling push the
first Saturday in December with 2 oz samples from 9 a.m. to noon.

## Black Friday Subscription Promo

**Offer: first bag free on any new subscription.** Runs Friday November 27 through
Monday November 30.

Reasoning: online subscriptions grew 34 percent in Q3 and it is our highest-margin
channel. Monthly churn is 6.2 percent, so a first-bag giveaway pays back inside
three months at current retention.

Promotion channels: email to our 8,400-person list (two sends, Friday morning and
Monday morning), in-store cards at every register, paid social Friday through
Sunday, and a homepage takeover.

Target: 250 net new subscribers over the four days. We added 330 in all of Q3, so
this is aggressive, but Black Friday coffee gifting is real.

## Rose City Books Partnership

Co-branded holiday gift set with Rose City Books, Portland only.

Set includes a 12 oz bag of the Ethiopia Yirgacheffe, a Wally's ceramic mug, and a
Rose City Books gift card. Retails at $42. Sold at the Rose City Books store on SE
Division, at our downtown flagship, and online with Portland-area shipping.

Split: we supply coffee and mugs at cost, Rose City Books supplies the gift cards and
retail space, and we split margin evenly. 500 sets for the first run.

This is the partnership I am most excited about. Two Portland institutions, the
audiences overlap heavily, and it costs us very little in cash.

## RTD Cold Brew Launch

Cans hit stores in November. Marketing is deliberately light here because the
first run is limited and we would rather sell through than create demand we cannot
meet.

- Case cards and cooler clings at all locations
- Sampling at the three mobile-ordering pilot stores only
- One email announcement, no paid spend
- Wholesale accounts get a heads-up from Priya two weeks ahead of retail

If the first run sells through by mid-December we go bigger in Q1.

## Social Media

**Cadence: 3 posts per week per channel** (Instagram, TikTok). Roughly:

- Monday: product or menu
- Wednesday: people, either staff or sourcing
- Friday/Saturday: community, locations, customers

**UGC campaign: #WallysCoffee.** Ask customers to post their drink or their bag.
Monthly drawing for a year of subscription (12 bags). Repost the best ones with
credit and permission. Budget covers the prizes and a small boost behind the top
performers.

Content we know works: Derek talking about a specific origin, latte art in
progress, the roaster running. Content that does not: generic quote graphics,
anything that looks like a stock photo.

## Measurement

Reporting to Wally monthly:

- New subscribers, by source
- Seasonal drink units, by location
- Gift set units sold
- Email open and click rate
- Follower growth and engagement rate
- RTD sell-through percentage

Q4 recap in the January leadership meeting.
