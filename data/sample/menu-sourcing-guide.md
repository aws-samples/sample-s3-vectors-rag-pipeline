## Where the Coffee Comes From

Four origins in rotation plus the new Sumatra. We buy through two importers and
one direct relationship, and Priya Rao handles most of the origin contracts.

### Ethiopia Yirgacheffe

Washing station in the Gedeb district, Yirgacheffe zone, through a cooperative of
roughly 240 smallholder farms. Heirloom varietals, washed process, grown between
1,900 and 2,200 meters. Tastes like bergamot, lemon peel, and jasmine, and it gets
floral as it cools.

Roast: light. First crack around 8:45, we drop at 9:30. No development past first
crack beyond about 45 seconds or the florals flatten out. This is our pour-over
default and the bag everyone reorders.

Available: August through February.

### Colombia Huila

Direct trade with Finca El Paraiso, outside Palermo in Huila. We have bought from
them for six years and Derek visits every other year. Caturra and Castillo,
washed, 1,750 meters. Caramel, red apple, and a heavier body than the Ethiopian.

Roast: medium. Drop at 10:45. Forgiving bean, hard to ruin, which is why it
anchors the house blend.

Available: year round, two harvests.

### Guatemala Antigua

Cafe Imports sources this from a group of mid-size farms in the Antigua valley.
Bourbon and Caturra, washed, volcanic soil at 1,600 meters. Cocoa, toasted
almond, a little bit of orange.

Roast: medium-dark. Drop at 11:30, just into second crack. Holds up in milk, so
this is most of what goes into espresso.

Available: January through August.

### Sumatra Lintong (new this year)

New for fall 2026, through Red Fox Coffee Merchants. Wet-hulled, from the Lintong
region near Lake Toba, 1,400 meters. Earthy, cedar, dark chocolate, very low
acidity. Divisive bean and that is fine, some people love it.

Roast: dark. Drop at 12:15. Wet-hulled coffee behaves differently in the roaster,
so Derek runs all Sumatra batches personally until the profile settles.

Available: September through January, first year so quantities are limited.

## Blends

**Wally's House** is 60 percent Colombia Huila and 40 percent Guatemala Antigua.
Medium roast. This is what goes in the batch brewers and most espresso drinks.

**Foghorn Espresso** is 70 percent Guatemala Antigua and 30 percent Sumatra
Lintong. Medium-dark and built for milk. It swapped in for the House on the
espresso side starting September.

**Decaf Twilight** is Colombian, Swiss Water process. Medium roast. Genuinely good
decaf and worth recommending, because most people expect it to be bad.

## Rotation Schedule

- **Spring (Mar-May):** Colombia, Guatemala, House, Decaf
- **Summer (Jun-Aug):** Colombia, Guatemala, Ethiopia arrives August, cold brew
  volume roughly triples
- **Fall (Sep-Nov):** Ethiopia, Colombia, Sumatra, Foghorn goes on espresso
- **Winter (Dec-Feb):** Ethiopia, Colombia, Sumatra, Peppermint Mocha returns

Green coffee lands at the Portland facility roughly every 3 weeks. Retail bags are
roasted to order twice a week, Tuesday and Friday, and we ship within 48 hours of
the roast date. The roast date is printed on every bag and customers do check it.
