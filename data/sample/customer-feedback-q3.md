# Q3 2026 Customer Feedback Summary

Compiled by Jordan Ellis from in-app surveys, Google reviews, and the feedback
cards at the register. 1,847 responses across all channels, which is up from 1,410
in Q2 mostly because we started prompting in the app.

## Net Promoter Score

**NPS: 72, up from 68 in Q2.**

By region:

| Region | NPS | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Bend | 81 | +3 |
| Eugene | 78 | +6 |
| Portland metro | 73 | +5 |
| Boise | 70 | +9 |
| Seattle | 66 | -2 |

Seattle is the only region that moved backward, and it lines up with the staffing
shortage and the reduced weekday hours. Several of the detractor comments mention
finding a store closed when they expected it open.

Boise jumped 9 points, which is the largest move. The kiosks opened to a lot of
skepticism about a walk-up format and appear to have won people over.

## What People Like

**Barista friendliness** was the single most-mentioned positive, appearing in 41
percent of promoter comments. It came up more often than the coffee itself, which
is worth sitting with.

**Single-origin pour-over.** Consistently praised, especially the Ethiopia
Yirgacheffe since it returned in August. Several comments specifically mention
staff explaining the origin, which suggests the sourcing guide training is landing.

**Oat milk as the default with no upcharge.** Still generating positive comments
more than a year after the change. A few people said it was the reason they
switched from a competitor.

**The 4-minute pour-over warning.** Small thing, mentioned repeatedly. People
appreciate being told the wait up front instead of discovering it.

## What People Complain About

**Mobile ordering wait times at Hawthorne.** The most common single complaint, 63
mentions. Orders are promised in 5 to 7 minutes and running 12 to 15 during the
morning peak. Hawthorne is not one of the three mobile pilot stores, it is on the
older BrewPoint ordering flow, and the queue does not throttle when the bar is
backed up. Sam is aware.

**Chai latte inconsistency.** 38 mentions across at least 9 locations. Same
complaint every time: it tastes different depending on where you buy it. We do not
have a standardized chai build, some stores steam the concentrate with milk and
some pour it over, and the ratio is not documented anywhere. This is entirely our
fault and it is fixable this quarter.

**Parking at Boise downtown.** 24 mentions. There is no fix here, the kiosk has no
lot and the street parking is metered and scarce. Worth noting for future site
selection rather than as an action item.

**Seattle hours.** 19 mentions about stores closing earlier than posted or being
closed unexpectedly. Directly tied to staffing.
