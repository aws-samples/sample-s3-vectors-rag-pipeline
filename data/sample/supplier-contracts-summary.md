# Supplier Contracts Summary

Maintained by Priya Rao (Director of Wholesale). Current as of September 2026.
This is a summary for internal reference, not the contracts themselves, which live
in the shared drive under Legal/Suppliers.

## Green Coffee

### Cafe Imports

Our largest importer by volume. Guatemala Antigua and most spot purchases.

- Contract runs through June 30, 2027, auto-renews annually unless we give 60 days
  notice
- Delivery every 3 weeks to the Portland facility, Tuesday arrival
- Minimum order 15 bags (69 kg each) per delivery
- Pricing tiers: under 40 bags per quarter at list, 40-80 bags at 4 percent off,
  over 80 bags at 7 percent off. We landed in the middle tier in Q3.
- Quality SLA: cup score within 1 point of the pre-ship sample, or we reject the
  lot and they cover return freight. We have exercised this twice in six years.
- Payment: net 30

### Red Fox Coffee Merchants

Newer relationship, added 2026 for the Sumatra Lintong.

- One-year agreement through August 31, 2027
- Delivery monthly, first week
- Minimum 8 bags per order, lower than Cafe Imports which is why we started here
  for a first-year origin
- No volume tier yet, revisit at renewal
- Quality SLA: pre-ship sample approval required before every lot ships
- Payment: net 15 for the first year, moves to net 30 on renewal

### Finca El Paraiso (direct trade)

Colombia Huila. Our only direct relationship and the one Derek cares most about.

- Six-year relationship, current agreement through the 2027 harvest
- Two shipments per year following the main and mitaca harvests, roughly April and
  October
- We commit to 55 bags annually at a fixed price of $3.40/lb FOB, which is well
  above the C-market and the entire point of the arrangement
- No minimum in the usual sense, since we commit to the volume up front
- Quality: we approve samples but there is no rejection clause. If a harvest is
  rough we work it out, which is what a six-year relationship buys you.
- Payment: 50 percent on shipment, 50 percent on arrival
- Derek visits every other year, next trip February 2027

## Equipment

### La Marzocco (espresso service contract)

Covers all 18 locations plus the two spare machines at the Portland facility.

- Annual service agreement, renews January 1
- Quarterly preventive maintenance visit per location, scheduled by region
- Annual rebuild per machine: gaskets, screens, steam valves
- Response time: 24 hours for a down machine in Portland and Seattle, 48 hours for
  Eugene, Bend, and Boise
- Loaner machine provided if a repair runs past 72 hours
- Annual cost: $34,200 across the fleet
- Contract number LM-PNW-2026-041
- Service line: (800) 555-0188

The 48-hour outlying response time has bitten us twice in Bend. Worth negotiating
at renewal.

## Packaging

### Cascade Container Co. (RTD cold brew cans)

New for the Q4 cold brew launch. First packaging contract we have signed.

- Initial term through December 31, 2027
- 12 oz aluminum cans, two SKUs: Honey Cardamom and Black
- Minimum order 25,000 units per SKU per run, which is the number that made this a
  real commitment rather than a test
- Lead time 6 weeks from artwork approval to delivery
- Pricing: $0.31 per can at 25,000 units, $0.27 at 50,000
- First run delivered October 22, 2026 for the November launch
- Quality: they cover defective cans (seam failures, print defects) at their cost
  if reported within 14 days of delivery

### Pacific Paper Supply (retail bags, cups, sleeves)

- Rolling agreement, 30 days notice to cancel either direction
- Delivery every two weeks to Portland, we distribute to stores
- No minimum, though pricing improves over $2,000 per order and we are usually
  over
- Payment: net 30
