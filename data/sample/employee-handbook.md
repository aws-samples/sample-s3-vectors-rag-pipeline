# Wally's Coffee Co. Employee Handbook

Last revised: August 2026. Questions go to your store manager first, then to Maya
Torres (VP of Operations).

## Our Locations

We run 18 cafes across the Pacific Northwest plus the roasting facility in
Portland. Three formats, and the procedures differ slightly:

- **Flagship (downtown Portland, SW 5th Ave)** carries the full food menu and runs
  6 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily, with a two-person open.
- **Neighborhood cafes** are the other 15 sit-down shops in Portland, Seattle,
  Eugene, and Bend. Pastry case only, 6:30 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- **Kiosk (Boise, both locations)** is espresso bar only, no seating, 6:30 a.m. to
  2 p.m. Single-person open is allowed at kiosks and only at kiosks.

## Opening

Whoever opens should be on the floor 45 minutes before doors (30 at the kiosks).

1. Disarm the alarm, lights on, unlock the back door only.
2. Turn the espresso machines on first. The Linea PB needs 25 minutes to come up
   to pressure, so this is step one, not step five.
3. Pull the temp log from the walk-in and the pastry case. Anything above 41F,
   write it down and call your manager before you sell it.
4. Dial in espresso. You get three shots to hit the window. If you cannot land it
   after three, switch to the backup grinder and put in a BeanTrack ticket.
5. Count the drawer. Starting float is $200 at cafes, $150 at kiosks.
6. Brew batch coffee at 6:00 (6:20 at neighborhood cafes). Batch gets dumped
   after 45 minutes, no exceptions.

## Closing

Doors lock at posted time. We do not serve after close, even if someone is
standing at the register.

1. Last batch brew goes out 45 minutes before close.
2. Backflush both groups, soak the portafilters and baskets.
3. Dish station, then floors, then the bathroom checklist on the clipboard.
4. Count the drawer, drop everything over the float in the safe. Two people sign
   the deposit slip at cafes. At the kiosks the closer signs and photographs it.
5. Set the alarm, out the back, check the door pulled shut behind you.

## Dress Code

Clean apron every shift, we launder them. Closed-toe non-slip shoes, and we will
reimburse up to $60 once a year with a receipt. Hair back if it passes your
shoulders. Small stud earrings are fine, no rings other than a wedding band
because they trap milk residue. Cover offensive tattoos, use your judgment and
your manager's if you are unsure. Ball caps are fine if they are a Wally's cap.

## Paid Time Off

PTO differs by status, and people get this wrong, so read the whole section.

**Full-time (30+ hours/week averaged over a quarter)**

- 0 to 2 years: 10 days per year
- 3 to 4 years: 15 days per year
- 5+ years: 20 days per year
- Accrues per pay period. You can carry 5 unused days into the next year, and
  anything past 5 is forfeited on Jan 1.

**Part-time (under 30 hours/week)**

- Accrues at 1 hour of PTO for every 30 hours worked, capped at 40 hours per year.
- No carryover for part-time. Use it in the calendar year or lose it.
- Eligible to start using accrued PTO after 90 days.

Request time off in Deputy at least 14 days ahead. December 1 through January 2
is a blackout for retail staff other than pre-approved holiday coverage.

## Food Safety

Everyone needs a food handler card within 30 days of your start date. We pay for
it. Wash hands entering the floor, between tasks, after the register, after
touching your face or phone. Sanitizer buckets get changed every 2 hours and at
every shift change, and yes, someone does check the test strips. Milk gets dated
when it is opened and pulled after 5 days or the printed date, whichever is
earlier. Your own drink lives on the under-counter shelf, never on the prep
counter. We got dinged for that in an inspection and it is an easy thing to
avoid.

## Cash Handling

No more than $300 in a drawer during a shift. Anything over that goes in the
safe, and you record the drop on the drawer sheet. Voids over $20 need a manager
code. If your drawer is off by more than $5 you write it up on the shift report
the same day. Being short happens and it is not a fireable thing on its own, but
hiding it is.

## Employee Discount

50 percent off drinks and food while you are on shift, and one free drink on any
day you work, on or off the clock. 30 percent off retail bags and merchandise,
up to 3 bags a month. The discount is yours, not your friends', and passing it
around is the fastest way to lose it.

## Social Media

Post about working here if you want, we like it. Do not post customer photos, the
inside of the safe, sales numbers, or anything from a leadership email. If a
reporter or a reviewer asks you for a comment about the company, be friendly and
send them to Maya. You are never obligated to answer.
