## Result

**Score: 96 out of 100. Pass.**

Routine unannounced inspection, inspector arrived at 10:40 a.m. on Wednesday
September 16, mid-morning rush. Walked the floor for about an hour and a half.
Two minor findings, both corrected, neither repeat. Previous inspection was
March 2026 at 94, so we moved up two points.

## Findings

### 1. Sanitizer concentration below threshold (dish station)

Quat sanitizer in the three-compartment sink tested at approximately 150 ppm
against a required 200-400 ppm range. The bucket had been made up at open and not
changed at the shift handoff, which is the actual root cause rather than anyone
mixing it wrong.

**Corrected same day.** Drained and remade in front of the inspector, retested at
just over 200 ppm. Also replaced the test strips at that station, since the open
container had been there long enough that I do not fully trust the readings we had
been taking.

Follow-up: shift leads now initial the sanitizer log at every changeover, not just
at open. Added a laminated card at the dish station with the ppm range on it,
because "200 to 400" is easier to hit when it is written where you are standing.

### 2. Employee beverage on prep counter

A closed employee drink was sitting on the prep counter next to the espresso
machine. Not an open container, and no product contamination, but it is a
violation regardless of the lid.

**Corrected immediately** by moving it. Retraining done the same afternoon with
the four people on shift, and I covered it again at the all-staff on September 20.
This one is in the handbook and we still got caught, which tells me the message
needs repeating rather than rewriting.

## What Passed Cleanly

Worth noting since the findings get all the attention:

- All cold holding at or below 41F. Walk-in was at 38F, pastry case at 39F.
- Temp logs complete for the full 90 days the inspector asked for
- Handwashing sinks stocked and accessible, no items stored in them
- All food handler cards current for staff on shift
- No pest activity
- Milk rotation and dating correct on every container checked
- Backflow prevention on the espresso water line verified

The inspector specifically noted the temp logs. Complete logs are boring and they
are also the thing that makes an inspection go smoothly.
