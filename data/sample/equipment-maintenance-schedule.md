# Equipment Maintenance Schedule

Owner: Maya Torres. Scheduling coordinated by Kenji Watanabe for Portland and by
each regional manager elsewhere. Covers all 18 cafes and the Portland roasting
facility.

Log every service visit in BeanTrack under the equipment record. If it is not
logged we cannot hold a vendor to the contract.

## Espresso Machines: La Marzocco Linea PB

Two-group at neighborhood cafes and the kiosks, three-group at the Portland
flagship. 20 machines total including the two spares at the roastery.

**Quarterly preventive maintenance.** Group gaskets inspected, screens and
dispersion plates cleaned, steam wands descaled, water filter checked, pressure
verified at 9 bar.

Regional schedule so the technician is not crossing the state twice:

| Region | Quarters |
|---|---|
| Portland metro (8) | Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct |
| Seattle (5) | Feb, May, Aug, Nov |
| Eugene (2) and Bend (1) | Feb, May, Aug, Nov |
| Boise kiosks (2) | Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec |

**Annual rebuild.** Gaskets, screens, steam valves, solenoids, and a full descale.
Scheduled in the store's slowest month, which is January or February for most
locations. Machine is down 4 to 6 hours, so it happens before open and the store
runs on the second group or takes a late open.

**Daily by staff:** backflush both groups at close, soak portafilters and baskets,
wipe steam wands after every drink.
**Weekly by staff:** detergent backflush, remove and scrub the screens.

Service: La Marzocco PNW, (800) 555-0188, contract LM-PNW-2026-041. Response is 24
hours in Portland and Seattle, 48 hours in Eugene, Bend, and Boise.

## Grinders: Mahlkonig EK43 and E65S

Each cafe runs an E65S on espresso and an EK43 for batch and pour-over. Kiosks run
one E65S only.

**Burr replacement every 12 months** or 1,200 kg of coffee, whichever comes first.
The flagship hits the weight threshold before the calendar, so track poundage in
BeanTrack rather than assuming a date.

Replacement months by region, staggered from the espresso service so we are not
down on both at once:

- Portland: March
- Seattle: April
- Eugene, Bend: April
- Boise: May
- Flagship: on weight, typically September and March

**Monthly:** hopper and chute cleaned with grinder tablets, calibration checked
against the reference dose.
**Daily:** brush the chute at close, wipe the hopper.

Burrs are ordered through Pacific Paper Supply on the equipment line. Keep one
spare set at the Portland facility so a worn set is never a multi-day wait.

## Roaster: Loring S35 Kestrel (Portland facility)

Single machine, and if it goes down the whole company stops shipping coffee. Treat
this schedule as non-negotiable.

**Annual full service every January.** Burner inspection and tuning, chaff
collection system stripped and cleaned, drum bearings checked, thermocouples
calibrated, software updated, safety interlocks tested. Two days down, scheduled
the second week of January when wholesale demand is lightest.

**Monthly:** chaff cyclone emptied and inspected, cooling tray perforations
cleared, exhaust ducting checked for buildup.
**Weekly:** drum and cooling tray cleaned, chaff collector emptied.
**Every roast day:** chaff emptied between batches, temp probes wiped.

Derek Lund signs off on all roaster maintenance personally.

Service: Loring factory-authorized technician out of Seattle, (206) 555-0173,
contract LOR-2026-S35-08. Emergency response 48 hours, which is the single largest
operational risk we carry. Ava has priced a used backup roaster and it is in the
2027 capital discussion.

## Refrigeration

Walk-ins at 12 locations. The smaller neighborhood cafes and both kiosks have no
room for one and run undercounter refrigeration only. Undercounter and pastry cases
at all 18.

**Semi-annual inspection**, April and October. Condenser coils cleaned, door
gaskets inspected, refrigerant checked, thermostat calibrated against a reference
thermometer, defrost cycle verified.

**Daily by staff:** temp logs twice per shift, at open and mid-afternoon. Anything
above 41F gets written down and reported before product is sold.

Service: Cascade Commercial Refrigeration, (503) 555-0155, contract CCR-18-2026.
Response 24 hours for a unit that is out, 4 hours if a walk-in is over 45F.

## Brewers and Water

**Batch brewers (Fetco CBS-2131XTS):** descale quarterly with the espresso PM
visit. Sprayhead cleaned weekly by staff.

**Water filtration (Everpure claim on the main line):** cartridge replaced every 6
months, April and October alongside refrigeration. Portland and Seattle water is
soft enough that we get the full interval, and Boise has run harder, so those two
kiosks are on a 4-month cartridge.

Water hardness tested quarterly at every location. Log it in BeanTrack. Scale is
the leading cause of espresso machine failure in our fleet and it is entirely
preventable.
